# 03. Test Lingvoyage on your phone

For everyone on the team. No coding and no computer setup: you'll use a free app called **Expo Go**, and Lingvoyage runs inside it.

Time: about 10 minutes, once. After that, opening Lingvoyage takes two taps.

---

## What you need

- An **iPhone** or **Android** phone
- The **email invite** from Expo to join **lingvoyage-team** (Ralph sends it)
- Wi-Fi or mobile data

---

## Step 1: Accept the Expo invite

1. Find the email from **Expo** inviting you to **lingvoyage-team**. Check spam if it isn't there.
2. Tap the button to accept.
3. If it asks you to sign up, create a free Expo account **using the same email address the invite was sent to**.
4. Remember this email and password. You need them in step 3.

No email? Ask Ralph to check which address the invite went to.

## Step 2: Install Expo Go

- **iPhone:** App Store > search **Expo Go** > Get
- **Android:** Play Store > search **Expo Go** > Install

## Step 3: Sign in to Expo Go

Open Expo Go and **log in** with the Expo account from step 1.

**Don't skip this.** Lingvoyage only opens for signed-in members of the team. Being signed out, or signed in with a different account, is the number one reason it doesn't open.

## Step 4: Scan the QR code

<img src="../setup/lingvoyage-qr.svg" alt="Lingvoyage QR code for Expo Go" width="220">

- **iPhone:** open the normal **Camera** app (not Expo Go), point it at the code, then tap the **Open in Expo Go** banner that appears.
- **Android:** open **Expo Go** > **Scan QR code**, and point it at the code.

Reading this on your phone? Open this page on a computer and scan the code from that screen, or ask a teammate to show it to you. If the image doesn't show, the code is also here: [qr.expo.dev link](https://qr.expo.dev/eas-update?projectId=8df178cb-b14c-46da-8d18-090c3317ad28&runtimeVersion=exposdk%3A57.0.0&channel=preview).

This QR code never changes. It always opens the newest version.

## Step 5: Make your Lingvoyage account

1. Lingvoyage opens inside Expo Go. The first load can take up to half a minute.
2. Tap **Get started** and sign up.
   - This is a **Lingvoyage** account, separate from your Expo account. You can use the same email.
   - Use a password you **don't** use anywhere else. There's no "forgot password" yet, so write it down.
3. When it asks for the **microphone**, tap **Allow**. The prompt says "Expo Go" instead of Lingvoyage; that's normal.

You're in. Try an Encounter: pick a scene and talk to the character, by voice or by typing.

---

## Opening it again later

Open **Expo Go** > tap **Lingvoyage** under **Recently opened**. No need to scan again.

## Getting the newest version

New versions arrive whenever the developers finish something. To get the latest:

1. **Fully close Expo Go.**
   - iPhone: swipe up from the bottom of the screen and pause, then swipe Expo Go up and away.
   - Android: open the recent apps view, then swipe Expo Go away.
2. Open Expo Go > **Lingvoyage**.
3. Still the old version? Close and reopen **once more**. The first open downloads the update, the second one shows it.

Your Lingvoyage account and progress stay the same between versions.

---

## Reporting a bug or an idea

Open an issue at **[github.com/ralphramosgit/lingvoyage/issues](https://github.com/ralphramosgit/lingvoyage/issues)** > **New issue**. You'll need a free GitHub account. No GitHub? Send the same details to Ralph in the team chat.

A good report has:

- **Title:** what went wrong, in a few words (for example: "Mic button does nothing on the hotel scene")
- **Steps:** what you tapped, in order
- **Expected:** what you thought would happen
- **What happened instead**
- **A screenshot or screen recording**
  - iPhone: Control Center > the record button (⏺)
  - Android: swipe down twice > **Screen record**
- **Your phone** (for example iPhone 15 or Pixel 8)

**Issues are public:** anyone on the internet can read them. Never put a password, your email or other personal details in one.

---

## Good to know

- **It's a test version.** Things will break. Finding them is the point.
- **Voice costs real money.** Every message to a character uses paid AI and voice services. Test as much as you like, just don't leave it running in a loop.
- **Don't share the QR code outside the team.** It won't open for people who aren't invited anyway.
- **When Expo Go updates itself,** Lingvoyage can stop opening for a while with a message about the "SDK version" until the team updates the app. Tell Ralph when that happens.

---

## Something's wrong?

| What you see | Try this |
|---|---|
| "No access", "project not found", or it won't load | In Expo Go, check you're **signed in** with the account the invite went to, and that you accepted the invite (step 1) |
| A message about the **SDK version**, or "incompatible with this version of Expo Go" | Update Expo Go from the App Store or Play Store. Still happening? Tell Ralph: the app needs an update on the team's side |
| The iPhone camera doesn't react to the QR code | Use the normal Camera app, not Expo Go. Get the whole code on screen and turn the screen brightness up |
| "Can't reach the Lingvoyage server" or a network error | Check your Wi-Fi or data, wait a minute and try again. If it keeps happening, tell Ralph |
| The character can't hear you | Allow the microphone. iPhone: **Settings** > **Expo Go** > **Microphone** on. Android: **Settings** > **Apps** > **Expo Go** > **Permissions** > **Microphone** > Allow |
| You can't hear the character | Turn the volume up, and on iPhone flip the silent switch off |
| Can't log in to Lingvoyage | Use the **Lingvoyage** account from step 5, not your Expo account. Forgot the password? Ask Ralph |
| Still the old version | Fully close Expo Go and reopen it, twice (see above) |
| A red or blank error screen | Screenshot it and report it (see above) |
